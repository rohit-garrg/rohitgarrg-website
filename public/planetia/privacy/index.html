Privacy Policy
Effective date: March 29, 2026
The short version: Planetia is designed for children. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

About Planetia

Planetia ("Solar System for Kids") is an educational iPad app for children ages 5 to 9, developed by Rohit Garg. The app is distributed through Apple's App Store under the Kids Category. This privacy policy applies to the Planetia app and any related services.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users, including children. Specifically:

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any other identifying information. We do not collect photos, videos, audio recordings, or files from your device. We do not collect location data. We do not collect device identifiers for the purpose of tracking or advertising. We do not use cookies, web beacons, or any similar tracking technologies.

Local Data Storage

Planetia stores app progress data (such as quiz scores, earned badges, and customization preferences) locally on your device and through Apple's iCloud Key-Value storage. This data is used solely to preserve the user's progress within the app and is never transmitted to us or any third party. This data is tied to the user's Apple ID and iCloud account, which is managed by Apple under their own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Planetia does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not communicate with any external services.

Advertising

Planetia contains no advertising of any kind. We do not display ads, sponsored content, or promotional material within the app.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Planetia is designed for and directed at children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international regulations concerning children's privacy.

Since we do not collect any personal information from any user, there is no personal information from children to disclose, sell, or share. We do not require account creation. We do not enable children to make personal information publicly available.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to any third party for any purpose.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and post the revised policy here. Since Planetia does not collect contact information, we cannot notify users directly of changes. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
